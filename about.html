About VoidBlue • v1.2

Shader Description

VoidBlue is a Minecraft shader project focused on realistic water and sky, with a strong blue atmosphere and a balance between visual quality and FPS.

Goal

Create a shader that looks clean, cinematic, and playable for survival, servers, and screenshots.

Style

Blue-themed atmosphere inspired by the void, calm skies, and reflective water.

Testing

Feedback is welcome for bugs, FPS drops, visuals, compatibility, and gameplay readability.

Message for testers/devs

Hey! VoidBlue v1.2 is now available, and snapshot 1.3b is planned for testing.

It’s focused on realistic water and sky while keeping a good balance between visuals and FPS.

If you have time, I’d really appreciate feedback about bugs, performance, visuals, or anything that could improve the shader.
